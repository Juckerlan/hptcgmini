How to Play

A two-player wizarding card game. Build a deck, manage Lessons, summon Creatures, cast Spells, and reduce your opponent's Health to zero.

1. Deck Building

Each player brings a saved, valid deck made of three separate components:

ComponentRequirement
Main DeckExactly 30 cards. Up to 3 copies of a card unless its individual restriction says otherwise.
Lesson DeckExactly 10 Lessons, kept separate from the Main Deck.
Starting AllyExactly 1 Starting Ally, selected separately from the Main Deck.
DomainsAt most 2 deck domains. Neutral cards do not count as a separate domain.

Use Deck Builder to create, validate, save, import, or export decks. The Database displays each card's printed cost, type, domain, and effect.

2. Game Setup & Mulligan

  1. Both players select and confirm a valid deck.
  2. Each player starts with 20 Health, their Starting Ally, and 4 Main Deck cards in hand.
  3. The game randomly chooses who takes the first turn, regardless of who hosts the room.
  4. The first player starts with 1 Lesson in play. The second player starts with 2 Lessons in play. These come from their own Lesson Decks.
  5. Each player may replace up to 2 cards during a one-time mulligan. Replaced cards are returned to the deck, which is shuffled before replacements are drawn.
  6. Once both mulligans are confirmed, the first player draws 1 card at the beginning of their turn. The second player does not draw until their first turn begins.
The second player's extra starting Lesson is a starting-position bonus, not an extra Lesson added to the 10-Lesson deck.

3. Turn Structure

  1. Turn Start: the active player draws 1 card from their Main Deck. The game displays TURN START and plays the start-of-turn chime.
  2. Assign Creature Damage: the game displays a reminder to assign Creature damage. The player resolves the appropriate damage manually.
  3. Play: take up to 2 actions, play cards, use effects, and respond to Spells as permitted.
  4. End Turn: press End Turn. The game plays a distinct end-of-turn chime, advances the active player's Ongoing Spell countdowns, and discards any that expire.
  5. Next Player: the other player's turn begins, including their turn draw and reminders.

Players cannot end a turn while a Spell Chain is still awaiting resolution. The game automatically tracks and limits Actions; actions reset to 2 at the beginning of each player's turn. Basic Cast has a dedicated button and its damage is applied manually.

4. Actions & Lessons

You have 2 actions per turn. Playing a Lesson costs an action (drawing one to your hand from an effect does not), and you may play no more than 2 Lessons per turn. Your total Lesson supply is limited to the 10 Lessons in your Lesson Deck.

Cards may also require Lessons to satisfy their printed cost and domain requirements. Lessons are your resource cards; use the card's printed information to determine whether you can play it.

Basic Cast is limited to once per turn and deals damage equal to the number of Allies you control. The simulator relies on players to track action usage and resolve most effects.

5. Card Types

Spells

Magical effects played through the Spell Chain. Each Spell requires an opponent's Resolve approval before it resolves.

Quick Spells

Spells that may be played in response to a pending Spell. They are added on top of the chain. The current rules also require discarding a Lesson as an additional cost for a Quick Spell; players must manage that cost manually.

Ongoing Spells

Spells that remain on the battlefield after resolving, often with a printed Countdown. Their effects are still activated and applied manually; their expiration is automated.

Creatures

Units with Health and Damage that remain on the battlefield. Some have special effects or Block.

Allies

Supporting characters with special abilities. The Starting Ally is chosen before the match. Playing an additional Ally costs 2 actions.

Items, Potions & Events

Play and resolve the card's printed effect. These each cost 1 action to play.

Locations

Cards representing places; playing a Location costs 2 actions.

Lessons

Cards from a separate 10-card deck that provide the resources needed for other cards.

6. Spell Chains & Resolve

  1. A player casts a Spell. It appears in the Spell Chain as pending.
  2. The opponent can play a Quick Spell in response, adding it above the original Spell, or approve Resolve.
  3. Players may continue responding with Quick Spells while the chain remains open.
  4. Spells resolve in last-in, first-out order: the most recently played Spell resolves first.
  5. Each Spell needs its own Resolve approval from the opponent of the caster.
  6. After resolution, ordinary Spells go to the discard pile; Ongoing Spells remain on the battlefield.

Example

Player A casts Expelliarmus. Player B responds with Protego. Player A approves Resolve for Protego first. Player B then approves Resolve for Expelliarmus.

Countdown

An Ongoing Spell with Countdown X may be used on the turn it is played and for X additional turns of its controller. Its countdown advances at the end of that controller's turns. It expires and is automatically discarded at the end of the last permitted turn, with a COUNTDOWN EXPIRED notice.

ExampleCountdownExpires
Glacius1End of its controller's next turn
Oppugno1End of its controller's next turn
Glacius Maxima2End of its controller's second following turn
The countdown controls how long the card remains in play; it does not automatically apply its printed effect. Damage, targets, Freeze, and other effects are still resolved by the players.

7. Creatures & Damage

Creatures have printed Health and Damage. Use their card text and the game state to assign damage, track damage and protection, and remove defeated Creatures.

At the start of a turn, the ASSIGN CREATURE DAMAGE reminder prompts the active player to perform the appropriate damage assignment. This is a reminder, not an automatic damage calculation.

Block: a blocking Creature protects its controller against applicable incoming damage; damage must be dealt with according to the Block rule before attacking the protected Player. The simulator does not automatically choose damage targets.

Basic Cast: once per turn, deal damage equal to the number of Allies you control, spending 1 action.

9. Creature Tokens

Tokens are generated by card effects, are not included in decklists, and disappear when they leave play.

Avis creates 2 Magical Birds when its Spell resolves. Piertotum Locomotor creates 2 Stone Guardians when its Spell resolves. Luna Lovegood can generate Luna's Thestral according to her ability.

10. Local & Online Matches

Local Play

Two players share one device and use Switch Side to alternate between seats. Spell Chains can be answered by switching players inside the chain window.

Online Private Rooms

Enter a player name, create or join a six-character private room, select a deck, and confirm. The host starts when both players are ready. The game randomly selects the first player, then both players complete their mulligans.

Players see the same battlefield state in real time. The opponent's hand is hidden visually. Use Chat to communicate and Action Log to review recorded actions.

Undo requires the opponent's approval online. Clicking an opponent's card briefly highlights it for approximately 2 seconds; clicking it again clears the highlight.

Online hands are hidden in the interface, but the current synchronization sends full game-state data between browsers. Use private rooms with trusted opponents. Reconnecting after a refresh is not yet guaranteed.
